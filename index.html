THE GAP

Running a model locally is not enough.

Product-grade local AI requires far more than weights on a device:

  • Runtime lifecycle: model management, compatibility & dynamic updates
  • Observability & telemetry: performance, latency & quality signals
  • Hardware adaptation: RAM/VRAM constraints & resident memory optimization
  • Developer ergonomics: unified APIs & intuitive user experience

VISION & MISSION

A local-first AI infrastructure layer for real-world products.

Vision: Local AI as the default compute substrate for privacy, sovereignty, and resilience.

Mission: Build fundamental infrastructure layers so builders can ship exceptional privacy-first AI experiences.
