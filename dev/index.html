LOCAL-FIRST AI INFRASTRUCTURE

Building the infrastructure
for privacy-first AI products.

I build the local AI foundations that let products understand, transcribe and reason without making the cloud the default destination for sensitive data.

Current work spans local LLM inference, private speech recognition and Android on-device runtimes.

Explore the three pillars →Follow the build journey ↗

DIRECTION

Make privacy-first AI products easier to build.

The infrastructure is a means to an outcome: useful products that keep sensitive workflows closer to users and under their control.

VISION

A future where useful AI runs close to personal data.

People and teams should be able to use powerful AI while retaining greater control over sensitive documents, conversations and personal information.

MISSION

Build reusable foundations for a new generation of products.

Create local inference, speech and mobile runtime layers that reduce the distance between an AI demo and a reliable privacy-first application.

  • Local by default
  • Privacy by architecture
  • Open and auditable

FROM LOCAL MODELS TO REAL PRODUCTS

Running a model locally is only the beginning.

Product-grade local AI needs the surrounding systems that make it dependable, measurable and simple enough to integrate into real user experiences.

  • 01
    Runtime lifecycle

    Model management, compatibility, updates and recovery.

  • 02
    Observability

    Latency, performance, health and quality signals.

  • 03
    Hardware adaptation

    Memory constraints, device profiles and thermal limits.

  • 04
    Product integration

    Stable APIs, packaging, privacy defaults and usable UX.
