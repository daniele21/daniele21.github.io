WHAT THIS PROVES

A useful financial workspace can keep the local ledger authoritative while automation remains reviewable and cloud continuity stays optional.

Aura separates deterministic financial decisions from platform-specific capture so convenience does not silently take ownership of the user's history.

Why Aura exists

A personal budget should help you decide.

Aura starts from a simple constraint: understanding your finances should not require a cloud backend to become the owner of your complete financial history.

01

What can I safely spend?

Budget, recorded spending and remaining days become a direct monthly signal.

02

Where is my money going?

Local reports show category totals, trends, comparisons and exceptional spending.

03

What still needs my decision?

Alerts, imports and detected Android payments stay visible until the user chooses what belongs in the ledger.

The goal is not more automation. It is more clarity without giving up ownership or control.
What changes

Useful finance software with fewer hidden trade-offs.

01

Local-first ownership

02

The idea

One local financial workspace.
Clear signals, explicit control.

Aura keeps the canonical ledger on the device, derives its insights through deterministic finance rules, and treats imports or detected payments as proposals until you confirm them.

Android-only capability

Faster capture. No silent bookkeeping.

On supported Android flows, Aura can recognize a likely payment locally and prepare a structured candidate. The transaction is created only after explicit review.

Under the hood

Local-first is an architecture boundary.

The shared React product and deterministic financial domain stay separate from Android-only capabilities, while cloud continuity remains optional and encrypted client-side.

React Native / Expo

Cross-platform core product, offline SQLite database, financial state engine and user experience.

Native Android Service

Background notification parsing, payment candidate detection and secure local IPC queue.

Client-Side Encryption

AES-256 encrypted payload serialization before optional Firebase cloud synchronization.

EVIDENCE & LIMITS

The current proof is about ownership, reviewability and explicit boundaries.

Aura demonstrates a local-first financial workspace and reversible automation. It does not turn those properties into unsupported claims about detection accuracy or financial outcomes.

EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

Implemented product boundaries are not outcome guarantees.

The current implementation supports a local source-of-truth ledger, deterministic finance rules, explicit review of Android payment candidates and optional client-side encrypted cloud continuity.

Supported today

  • Local workspace remains the canonical source of truth
  • Safe-to-spend and reporting derive from deterministic recorded data
  • Detected Android payments require explicit review before recording
  • Optional cloud payloads are encrypted client-side before synchronization

Not claimed here

  • Comparative payment-detection accuracy benchmark
  • Measured financial-outcome improvement versus alternative budgeting tools
